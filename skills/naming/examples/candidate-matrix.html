Download concurrency bound candidate matrix

Compare three names for the maximum number of simultaneous downloads. This original design example weighs guide terminology against clarity in isolated keywords and scheduler comparisons. Selection status: proposed, awaiting reader review.

Assumptions

  • Behavior (E1): A positive integer bounds simultaneous downloads. Admission requires occupied slots below the bound; reservation precedes start, and completion releases the slot.
  • Vocabulary (E2): The draft heading is "Download concurrency." Its definition states the maximum number of simultaneous downloads, with a minimum of one.
  • Audience (E3): Engineers read isolated keywords and receiver-qualified fields. Both contexts matter equally in this design packet.
  • Scope (E4): The field is unpublished. Neighboring names are downloads_in_progress and download_timeout_seconds.

Open Questions

  • Which reader context deserves priority? Observed use frequency or reader feedback could break the equal-weight tie in either direction.
  • Does implementation preserve the design contract? Inspect admission, slot reservation and release, declarations, and exposed representations before applying the name.

Eligibility and scoring

All three candidates describe E1's bound under E2's definition and use the draft's full-word convention. downloads_in_progress is excluded: E4 reserves that spelling for current occupancy. A preference score cannot override that conflict. Complexity, performance, coupling, testability, reversibility, and blast radius are equal for these unpublished spelling alternatives.

Each weight is 1 because E3 supplies no priority among the reading tasks. Scores are local judgments: 1 is weak fit, 3 adequate or mixed fit, and 5 strong fit. Each row gives concrete anchors. Decision

Proposed: maximum_concurrent_downloads. Its explicit count bound fits isolated keywords and the comparison with current occupancy. Equal weights tie it with the draft at 16; preferring those two contexts is an explicit naming judgment awaiting reader review.

The current download_concurrency preserves exact guide wording and a shorter receiver-qualified expression. Giving guide lookup weight 2 yields totals 21, 15, and 19, favoring that draft. Giving isolated-bound clarity weight 2 instead yields 19, 15, and 21, favoring the proposed maximum. concurrent_download_limit remains viable but has neither advantage under these anchors.

Distinct contextual review

Static review of E1 confirms the described boundary: with bound 4, occupied counts 0 and 3 permit admission; 4 requires waiting for a released slot. E4 distinguishes the bound from current occupancy and duration. The three sample pairs preserve that interpretation. Outcome: description-level fit supported for maximum_concurrent_downloads.
