# Naming decision

Owner: [selection](../references/selection.md#record-a-clear-winner-directly).
Complete the core fields, using a paragraph for a small settled choice. Link
existing records where they supply the evidence.

## Core record

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Concept and context | {Thing or behavior being named, artifact identity, audience, and relevant scope; link the brief or provide its evidence and constraints.} |
| Outcome | {Recommended or retained spelling, or unresolved decision; show the name in a representative declaration and use.} |
| Selection status | {Proposed / accepted / unresolved. For acceptance, record the actual authority, decision, and evidence.} |
| Rationale | {Behavior, vocabulary, and use-site evidence supporting the choice; decisive constraints or comparison criteria.} |
| Assumptions and unknowns | {Remaining uncertainty, impact on the choice, and evidence needed; state none only within the supported scope.} |
| Verification | {Separate contextual review outcome, examined evidence, coverage limits, and report link; use pending when review has yet to occur.} |

## Conditional details

- Alternatives and tradeoffs: {Link the [comparison](candidate-comparison.md),
  or explain the meaningful alternative and why the recommendation is preferred.}
- Reopening condition: {New evidence, changed audience priority, or changed
  contract that would require another decision.}
- Human review: {Requested reviewer, scope, actual status, and received judgment
  or pending feedback.}
- Applied rename: {Actual authorization and scope, [rename plan](rename-plan.md),
  edited revision, and [applied-change checks](validation-report.md#applied-change-checks).}

Use [contextual validation](../references/contextual-validation.md) to assess the
chosen or existing name and [renaming](../references/renaming.md) to carry out an
authorized edit.
