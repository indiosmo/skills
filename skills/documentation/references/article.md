# Writing a technical article

A technical article develops an argument or account for a defined audience.
Use the [article template](../templates/article.md) and
[worked example](../examples/document-types/article.md). The
[Write the Docs writing guide](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/writing/beginners-guide-to-docs/)
helps establish the reader's needs; Google's
[cross-reference guidance](https://developers.google.com/style/cross-references)
helps make supporting sources useful.

## Build the argument

State the question, audience and thesis. Organize the body around claims and
evidence, using examples to make the reasoning concrete. Distinguish an observed
result from a general conclusion and identify limits or competing explanations.
A procedural digression can link to a how-to; a long lookup table can link to
reference.

Attribute ideas, quotations, data and expert accounts to their actual sources.
Verify permissions before reproducing material and keep quotations proportionate
to the purpose. Record revision, date and scope when results depend on them;
use the [evidence register](evidence.md) for review-only provenance.

## Review

Check that each section develops the argument for its intended reader. Verify
facts and citations, assess counterevidence, and qualify conclusions to the
available observations. Review terminology, example meaning and the distinction
between evidence and opinion. Obtain relevant human review for factual and
release-sensitive claims.
