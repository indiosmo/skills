# Compare complete and unterminated samples

This fictional Text Workshop activity takes you through two pages. You will
inspect a prepared sample, count it, and compare a second sample with different
newline boundaries.

You need a terminal, Bash 4.4 or later, wc and cat, plus the checked-in evidence
directory. Run every command from the parent document-types directory, the one
containing [the product README](../readme.md).

1. [Inspect and count the complete sample](01-complete-sample.md).
2. [Compare the unterminated sample](02-unterminated-sample.md).

The group ends when you have observed counts of 2 and 1 and connected the
difference to the sample endings.
